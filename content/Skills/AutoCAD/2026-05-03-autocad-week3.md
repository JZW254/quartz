**Key Learnings This Week**

- Improved ability to read and interpret CAD drawings:
  - When a line length is not annotated, check parallel lines in horizontal or vertical directions for reference
  - When an angle is missing, look for parallel lines or use the Offset command to assist
- Learned several practical drawing techniques:
  - Quick methods to draw diagonal lines
  - How to draw accurate positioning lines (定位線)
  - Techniques for drawing tangents (切線)

**Important Improvements**
- Realized that not all lines need individual annotations — identical lengths are often marked only once
- Developed a better drawing workflow:

**Weekly Reflection**

This week I continued with the second training course. The teaching style of the second instructor is quite different from the first. 

With 17 years of CAD experience, he is highly skilled and practical. For every set of commands he taught, he immediately provided at least two exercises and demonstrated how to apply them. Most valuably, he not only showed **how** to do something, but also explained **why** that method is better or faster. He also generously shared real-world professional insights, such as how to read annotations and important drawing principles.

Comparing the two teachers: the first one excels at guiding complete beginners with detailed explanations of each command, while the second focuses more on practical application and real-workflow thinking. Both are excellent in their own ways. Spending two days searching for these courses was definitely worth it.
