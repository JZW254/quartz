**Weekly Reflection**

This week I spent a significant amount of time trying to master orthographic projections. However, progress was slower than expected. After watching multiple tutorial videos, I realized that many instructors rely heavily on experience and intuition rather than clear, logical step-by-step methods. This approach works well for them but is difficult for beginners like me to replicate quickly.

After careful consideration, I made a strategic decision: instead of spending too much time struggling with areas where I am weak, I will focus on perfecting the topics I can already handle well and aim to score as high as possible in those sections. While this means giving up some difficult questions, I believe this pragmatic approach gives me the best chance of passing the exam within the remaining time.

This experience has also taught me an important lesson about exam preparation: understanding the exam structure and scoring distribution early is crucial for making smart study decisions.
