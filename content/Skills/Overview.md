# Skills Overview 👷‍♂️

Hello / Hallo to everyone visiting this page! 👋

### What is this page about?
This section is dedicated to tracking my skills development in a clear and structured way. It covers both my current professional skills and the new technical skills I am actively learning as I transition toward becoming an **Automation Technician**.

### Why am I creating this section?
Most of my skills so far have been gained passively through daily work. While I’ve learned a lot, I often struggle to remember exactly *when*, *why*, and *how* I acquired each skill — or what results I achieved with them. This sometimes makes my growth feel vague and uncertain.

Now that I’ve decided to change my career path at 30, I see this as the perfect opportunity to shift from **passive** to **active** learning, and from **disordered** to **structured** progress.

It’s never too late to start — better late than never! 💪

### How will I track my skills going forward?
I really like the **PDCA** (Plan-Do-Check-Act) methodology from ISO 9001. The best way to show respect for a good method is to actually use it. Therefore, I will organize all my future learning using the PDCA cycle:

**Plan**  
- Set clear goals using **SMART** principles (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)  
- Prepare study materials, tools, and schedule  
- Decide how much time I will invest daily/weekly

**Do**  
- Execute the plan consistently  
- Record daily/weekly progress  
- Document questions and challenges

**Check**  
- Review whether I met my goals (weekly, monthly, quarterly)  
- Write honest reflections on what worked and what didn’t

**Act**  
- Adjust the plan based on results  
- Prepare for the next learning cycle

### My Goals

**Short-term (Next 1–2 years)**  
- Systematically document how I acquire new skills  
- Build a clear, visible record of my progress that future employers can see  
- Successfully transition from Assembly Electrician to Automation Technician

**Long-term**  
- Continue lifelong learning  
- Live a fulfilling and high-quality life through continuous self-improvement ✨
