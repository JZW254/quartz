# 2026 Week 18: German Self-Learning Week 1 Summary

**Overall Learning Goal**  
Reach A1 level and pass the German A1 language test by the end of December 2026.

**This Week’s Input**  
- Studied 7 days  
- Daily study time: 2 hours  
- **Total: 14 hours**  
- Overall progress: ~5%

**Key Learnings This Week**

- Completed two online German pronunciation courses (free resources)
- Learned basic German pronunciation rules
- Created 54 Anki cards focused on pronunciation rules and common words

**Important Improvements**
- Gained a clearer understanding of fundamental pronunciation rules, including:
  - The difference between long and short vowels and when to use each
  - The distinction between stressed and unstressed syllables

**Challenges & Solutions**

- **Challenge**: Some letters and letter combinations (such as “au” and “ai”) are pronounced slightly differently across various tutors and resources.  
- **Solution**: 
  1. Watch more pronunciation videos from different sources  
  2. Use online dictionaries with real German audio as a reliable reference

**Next Week’s Plan**
- Complete two additional pronunciation courses
- Finish organizing all Anki cards related to pronunciation
- If time allows, start listening to “Coffee Break German” materials

**Weekly Reflection**

At the beginning of this week, I thought German pronunciation was quite difficult. However, after completing two structured courses, I started to see patterns and rules. I believe one more week of focused practice will make me much more comfortable with this part. After that, the main task will shift to consistent practice and listening.
